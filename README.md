# Daniel Challenger

**Data Engineer | Industrial Telemetry & IoT | Predictive Maintenance**

I turn messy, high-frequency operational data into something reliable and usable.

At [Airth](https://airth.io), I process **~400,000 telemetry records per day** from GPS and equipment sensors in mining operations, collected at 2-second to 1-minute intervals. I helped build a real-time water truck tracking system that contributed to **reducing water usage by millions of gallons per month** while maintaining dust control.

My background is in **Mining & Metallurgical Engineering** (University of Arizona), so I understand the equipment and operations behind the data, not just the data itself.

## 🔧 What I work on

- **Data pipelines & data quality:** cleaning and validating high-frequency sensor and GPS data at scale
- **Predictive maintenance:** survival analysis, time-to-event modeling, and failure-risk prediction
- **Operational analytics:** equipment behavior, queue times, and fleet utilization
- **Dashboards & reporting:** making data usable for people running the operation
